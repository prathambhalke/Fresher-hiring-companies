## Why apply directly?
 
- Applications go straight to the company's own hiring system.
- Openings are usually posted on official career pages first.
- You avoid outdated or duplicate listings on third-party portals.

## Tips for freshers
 
1. **Keep your resume to one page** and tailor it to each role.
2. **Build 2-3 solid projects** and host them on GitHub with clear READMEs.
3. **Practice aptitude and coding basics**, since most service companies start with an online assessment.
4. **Set up job alerts** on each company's career page.
5. **Apply early in the hiring cycle**, as many drives fill up fast.
6. **Beware of scams**: genuine companies never charge a fee for interviews or offers.
